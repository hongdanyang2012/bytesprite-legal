Help & Support

Support page for store listings

For account, subscription, backup, or privacy help, contact us via Help & Feedback inside the AuthNest app.

Do not include TOTP secrets, OTPs, PINs, or plaintext passwords in support messages.

  • Restore after switching devices: sign in with the same account and use encrypted cloud backup restore.
  • Subscriptions: manage via Google Play / App Store, or use Restore Purchases in the app.
  • Delete account: follow Account settings in the app.
