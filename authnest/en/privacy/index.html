AuthNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-14

AuthNest (“we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information related to the AuthNest mobile app.

Encryption and local-first design

  • Authenticator secrets (TOTP) and password vault plaintext stay in the device secure area (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) with local encryption.
  • When cloud backup is enabled, the client packs data and encrypts it with AES-256-GCM before upload.
  • The cloud stores ciphertext only. Our servers do not decrypt secrets, parse secrets, generate OTPs, or read password plaintext.
  • App-lock PINs are stored locally as hashes and are never uploaded.

Information we process

  • Account: email or third-party sign-in identifiers (e.g. Apple / Google) for cloud backup and membership.
  • Backup ciphertext: encrypted snapshots and metadata (version, checksum, entry counts) after you opt in to cloud backup.
  • Optional settings mirror: theme / language / lock timeout — off by default; synced only if you enable it.
  • Subscription status: synced via Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat for entitlements and quotas.
  • Optional diagnostics: crash logs, if enabled, are redacted and never include secrets, OTPs, PINs, or backup plaintext.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect or upload TOTP secrets, one-time passwords (OTPs), or vault passwords in plaintext.
  • We do not ship third-party advertising SDKs by default and do not use ATT for ads.

Third parties

  • Supabase: authentication and encrypted backup storage.
  • RevenueCat and Apple / Google: subscriptions and receipt validation.

These providers process data under their own policies, limited to what is needed for AuthNest features.

Retention and deletion

  • You control local data; uninstalling removes on-device data (unless retained by system backups).
  • After disabling cloud backup or deleting an account, we stop retaining related cloud ciphertext per product flow and applicable law.
  • Contact us via in-app Help & Feedback for account-related requests.

Children

AuthNest is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

Contact

Use in-app Help & Feedback for privacy questions. Do not send secrets, OTPs, PINs, or plaintext passwords.
