Delete your AuthNest account

Developer: bytesprite · App: AuthNest: 2FA & TOTP (cn.bytesprite.authnest)

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open AuthNest and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings > Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email bytespirit.support@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, using the subject AuthNest account deletion request. We may reply to confirm you own the account before deleting it. Never send TOTP secrets, codes, PINs, or passwords.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email and authentication records).
  • Your profile and app preferences synced to the cloud.
  • Your encrypted cloud backup (TOTP accounts and vault entries).
  • Subscription entitlement and usage quota records linked to your account.

Codes stored only on your device stay on that device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Export them first if you still need them.

What may be kept, and for how long

Deletion is completed within 30 days of a verified request. Purchase records held by Google Play are managed by Google, not by us. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

Delete data without deleting your account

Turn off cloud backup in the app to remove your encrypted backup from our servers while keeping your account.
